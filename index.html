01 · Situation

CHAGEE is winning product. The next battle is narrative.

🚀

Rapid Expansion

Six active APAC markets in 2025 — Malaysia, Singapore, Thailand, Indonesia, Philippines, Vietnam — with 300 net new locations in Q3 alone. Speed creates communication gaps that competitors will exploit.

🫖

Brand Perception Risk

91% revenue concentration in the tea latte category is a strength and a vulnerability. CHAGEE must own the "premium oriental tea" narrative before local and Western competitors do.

🌏

Cultural Translation

Yunnan heritage and 霸王茶姬 brand equity don't translate automatically. Each APAC market demands a distinct localisation strategy — from Malaysia's Telepuk campaigns to Singapore's premium lifestyle positioning.

📋

Regulatory Complexity

F&B licensing, halal certification, food labelling standards, and foreign brand regulations vary across six markets. Government affairs is not optional — it is a market entry prerequisite.

📰

Media Fragmentation

APAC media is fractured across English, Mandarin, Bahasa, Thai, Filipino, and Vietnamese. Coordinating consistent messaging across this landscape without a regional comms function is not sustainable.

📈

Post-IPO Scrutiny

Listed on NASDAQ in April 2025, CHAGEE is now a public company. Investor relations, ESG narrative, and media accuracy are non-negotiable. Reputation risk has a direct share price dimension.

02 · Strategy

Four pillars. One coherent APAC voice.

The strategic objective is simple: make CHAGEE the undisputed premium modern tea authority in APAC — not just the biggest tea brand, but the most culturally trusted one.

Every market is different. But the brand voice must be recognisable across all of them. This strategy builds a regional comms architecture that is consistent at the top and locally intelligent at the edges — giving country teams clear frameworks while preserving the creative latitude to resonate.

Cultural Authority

Position CHAGEE as the global ambassador of modern oriental tea culture, not merely an F&B brand.

  • Heritage storytelling from Yunnan to APAC
  • Cultural collaboration campaigns per market
  • Press trips to Yunnan for regional editors
  • Annual Modern Oriental Tea Summit

Media Velocity

Build a proactive regional media engine that gets ahead of launches, issues, and competitor narratives.

  • 24-hour response standard for all media
  • Monthly regional PR cadence calendar
  • Lifestyle, F&B, and business media split
  • Flagship outlet editorial partnerships

Government Trust

Build durable relationships with regulators, trade bodies, and ministries before crises occur.

  • Market-by-market stakeholder maps
  • Proactive halal and food safety briefings
  • Industry association memberships
  • Country team GR support protocols

Issues Readiness

Maintain a live issues register and pre-built response playbooks so no crisis catches the team unprepared.

  • Quarterly risk horizon scans per market
  • Pre-approved holding statement library
  • 24/7 escalation protocol by market
  • Monthly brand sentiment monitoring
03 · Priority Markets

Every market has its own story.

🇲🇾
Flagship

Malaysia

CHAGEE's first and most mature international market. 200+ tea houses. Strategy here is about maintaining brand prestige, deepening Malay and Chinese media relationships, and managing the halal certification narrative. Telepuk cultural campaign is a model for regional replication. Focus: BFM Radio, Media Permata, Borneo Bulletin, Bernama.

🇸🇬
Established

Singapore

Premium lifestyle positioning is key. Singapore media is English-first, demanding, and sceptical of hype. Invest in Straits Times, CNA, and SGAG relationships. Leverage Singapore's role as APAC comms hub. Government engagement via STB, ESG, and SFA for food safety trust-building. The Singapore market sets the tone for regional investor and trade media.

🇹🇭
Established

Thailand

Strong local tea culture creates both opportunity and complexity — CHAGEE must differentiate from Thai tea. Social-first media strategy: Thai influencer ecosystem is powerful. Government relations with the FDA and Ministry of Commerce are priority for expansion permits. Bangkok-centric media with National and Post English outlets as key targets.

🇮🇩
Growth

Indonesia

Largest APAC market by population. Muslim-majority — halal positioning is non-negotiable and a genuine competitive advantage. Antara and Jakarta Post are key wire relationships. BPOM regulatory navigation critical. Local language content (Bahasa Indonesia netspeak) essential for social and influencer channels. Target Tier 1 Jakarta malls for grand opening PR.

🇵🇭
Growth

Philippines

Exceptional launch momentum — grand opening drew massive crowds August 2025. High social media affinity market. Philippine Star, Manila Bulletin, and ABS-CBN online are priority media targets. FDA Philippines engagement for product approvals. Local cultural tie-ins (Filipino hospitality and community) will amplify brand warmth narratives.

🇻🇳
Emerging

Vietnam

Newest market entry. Vietnam's café culture and young demographic are a natural fit. Media strategy: Thanh Nien, VnExpress for credibility. Social strategy: Zalo and TikTok Vietnam are essential. Ministry of Industry and Trade engagement for franchise licensing. Cultural authenticity messaging must be sensitive to Vietnam-China relationship dynamics.

Lifestyle & F&B

Product launches, grand openings, and cultural campaigns require lifestyle writers and F&B specialists who can generate premium editorial coverage — not just product mentions. Target: food and lifestyle verticals of all major dailies plus dedicated F&B publications per market.

Business & Investor

As a NASDAQ-listed company, CHAGEE's expansion milestones, GMV growth, and ESG commitments are legitimate business news. Bloomberg, Reuters, and business desks of major APAC broadsheets need a dedicated communications track separate from consumer PR.

Government & Trade

Regulatory approvals, halal certification milestones, community hiring commitments, and local sourcing partnerships generate positive coverage in trade and government-adjacent media — critical for relationship-building with ministries and trade associations.

06 · Government Affairs

Regulatory trust is a competitive moat.

Stakeholder Mapping

Build and maintain a live stakeholder map across all six APAC markets, covering food safety regulators, halal certification bodies, trade ministries, industry associations, and local government partners.

  • SFA Singapore — food safety and import licensing
  • JAKIM Malaysia — halal certification authority
  • BPOM Indonesia — food and drug regulatory body
  • FDA Thailand and Philippines — product approvals
  • Ministry of Industry and Trade Vietnam — franchise licensing
  • ASEAN Business Advisory Council — regional advocacy

Regulatory Vulnerability Radar

Track legislative and regulatory movements across markets that could affect CHAGEE's operations — sugar tax legislation, foreign brand franchise rules, social media advertising laws, and food labelling updates.

  • Monthly regulatory scan per market — flagged to leadership
  • Pre-emptive minister and ministry briefings on product health credentials
  • Localisation of health and wellness narrative per regulatory environment
  • Crisis protocol for food safety incidents — 24-hour response standard
  • Annual government stakeholder appreciation events per market
  • ESG reporting alignment with local CSR expectations
